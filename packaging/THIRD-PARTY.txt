Other open-source parts it uses. The app's settings page (About > Open-source libraries) lists every Rust library
with its version, licence and source.

libmpv (video player), GPL-2.0-or-later
  Windows packages ship libmpv-2.dll, which contains FFmpeg and other libraries.
  Source: https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv (commit e471496c56)
  Build recipe: https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake (tag 20261021)
  Linux and macOS use the system's libmpv.

FFmpeg (camera and voice notes on Linux), LGPL-2.1-or-later or GPL-2.0-or-later depending on the build
  Used as the system's ffmpeg program. Source: https://ffmpeg.org

ALSA (audio on Linux), LGPL-2.1-or-later
  Loaded from the system. Source: https://github.com/alsa-project/alsa-lib

Fontconfig (fonts on Linux), MIT-style licence
  Source: https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/fontconfig/fontconfig

libopus (voice notes), BSD-3-Clause
  Built into the app. Source: https://github.com/xiph/opus

Fonts, SIL Open Font License 1.1
  Inter, Syne, Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono, Manrope, Sora, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Poppins, Lora, Merriweather,
  PT Serif, EB Garamond, Atkinson Hyperlegible, Lexend, Space Mono, Quicksand, Noto Sans JP, OpenDyslexic.
  The licence texts are in assets/fonts in the source code.
